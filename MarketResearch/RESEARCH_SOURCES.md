## 1. Vietnam & Ho Chi Minh City Pickleball Boom (2024-2026)

### 1.1 Participation, Awareness & Global Velocity
1. **UPA Asia & YouGov Singapore Joint APAC Pickleball Study (2025/2026)**:
   - **National Awareness**: **88%** of surveyed Vietnamese respondents have heard of pickleball - ranking **#1 in Asia-Pacific** across 12 surveyed territories (ahead of Singapore at 70%).
   - **Trial & Active Player Base**: Over **37%** of urban/surveyed Vietnamese have tried pickleball at least once, with an estimated **16 million frequent players** (playing $\ge 1\times$/month) nationwide, making Vietnam the fastest-growing pickleball market in Asia (+152% awareness surge YoY).
   - **DUPR (Dynamic Universal Pickleball Rating) Adoption**: Vietnam ranks in the **Global Top 10** for DUPR registered player growth velocity, driven by rapid cross-over from badminton, tennis, and table tennis.
   - **Reference Coverage**: *Dan Tri* (`https://dantri.com.vn`), *VOV* (`https://vov.vn`), *The Picklebase* (`https://thepicklebase.com`), *UPA Asia / YouGov APAC Report*.

2. **Metric.vn E-Commerce Retail & Equipment Market Report (2024-2026)**:
   - **2024 Spark**: Online sales of pickleball paddles, balls, shoes, and court apparel scaled from near-zero in Q1 2024 to **~272 billion VND** across Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada, and Tiki in full-year 2024.
   - **2025 Hyper-Growth (+1,282% YoY)**: In H1 2025 alone, Vietnamese shoppers spent **511 billion VND (~$20M USD)** on pickleball equipment (+1,282% vs. H1 2024), with full-year 2025/2026 retail equipment & apparel crossing the **1,000 billion VND (~$40M USD)** milestone (>1.4 million paddles sold).
   - **Reference Coverage**: *Vietnam News* (`https://vietnamnews.vn`), *VnExpress* (`https://vnexpress.net`), *Metric.vn Big Data E-Commerce Reports*.

3. **2026 Institutionalization & Global Tournaments in Vietnam**:
   - **Heineken Pickleball World Cup 2026 (Da Nang, Aug 30 - Sep 6, 2026)**: First Pickleball World Cup hosted in Asia (Tien Son Sports Arena & Tuyen Son Sports Village), drawing nearly **5,000 athletes from >80 countries**, setting a Guinness World Record for participation scale, with Team Vietnam winning the historic **Silver Medal** in the National category final against the United States.
   - **PPA Tour Asia - MB Ho Chi Minh City Open & Vietnam Cup**: Flagship professional & amateur tour stops hosted at *The Global City Sports Park (Thu Duc / HCMC)* and *Da Nang*, featuring $50,000+ USD pro prize pools and massive amateur age/skill brackets.
   - **10th National Sports Festival (HCMC, 2026) & Vietnam Pickleball Federation (VPF)**: Official inclusion of pickleball as a medal sport in Vietnam's National Sports Festival hosted in Ho Chi Minh City.
   - **Reference Coverage**: *PPA Tour Asia* (`https://ppatour-asia.com`), *VietnamNet* (`https://vietnamnet.vn`), *Lao Dong* (`https://laodong.vn`), *Sai Gon Giai Phong* (`https://sggp.org.vn`).

## 2. TAM - SAM - SOM Sizing Methodology (`2024` to `2029`)

All market sizing figures in `configs/market.toml` and `tam_sam_som_market_sizing.csv` are reported in **Billion VND / year (`B VND/yr`)** and reconcile bottom-up court economics with top-down industry data:

### 2.1 Total Addressable Market (`TAM`: Vietnam National Pickleball Ecosystem)
- **2026 Current TAM = `2,450.0B VND/yr`** (scaling at **$28.35\%$ CAGR** from `2026` to **`5,180.0B VND/yr` in `2029`**):
  1. **National Court Hire & Club Memberships (`980.0B VND/yr` in 2026)**: ~3,200 active commercial courts nationwide (1,050+ in HCMC, 200+ in Hanoi, plus Da Nang, Binh Duong, Can Tho, Hai Phong, Nha Trang, Vung Tau) generating an average of ~306M VND/court/year in gross court hire and local club passes.
  2. **Equipment, Paddles, Balls & Court Apparel Retail (`820.0B VND/yr` in 2026)**: Grounded in Metric.vn's ~511B VND H1 2025 e-commerce tracking plus offline pro-shop and distributor sales.
  3. **Coaching, Academies & Junior Clinics (`360.0B VND/yr` in 2026)**: Private and group coaching across commercial venues (~22-25% of total venue spend).
  4. **Courtside F&B, Social Mixers & Amateur Tournaments (`290.0B VND/yr` in 2026)**: Specialty drinks, hydration, and sponsor-backed amateur brackets.

### 2.2 Serviceable Available Market (`SAM`: Ho Chi Minh City Commercial Pickleball Venue, Coaching, F&B & Membership Market)
- **2026 Current HCMC SAM = `1,120.0B VND/yr`** (`1,120,000M VND/yr`), expanding at **$24.18\%$ CAGR** to **`2,145.0B VND/yr` in `2029`**:
  - **Bottom-Up Court Verification**: HCMC operates **~1,050 active courts across ~165 commercial venues** in 2026 (scaling to **~1,650 courts across ~240 venues by 2029**).
  - At an HCMC commercial average of `1,066.7M VND / court / year` across court hire (`655.0B VND`), coaching (`215.0B VND`), courtside F&B (`165.0B VND`), and rentals/events/memberships (`85.0B VND`), total HCMC commercial venue spend equals **`1,120.0B VND/yr`**. (Note: PICK!'s baseline 6-court venue generates `7,680M VND / 6 courts = 1,280.0M VND / court / year`, or `+20.0%` above the HCMC average due to its `42,000`-follower social funnel).
  - **Prime Urban HCMC Catchment SAM (`Thao Dien / Thu Duc`, `Binh Thanh`, `Phu My Hung / District 7`, and `<3 km` Local Radius)**: Represents **`142.0B VND/yr` in 2026**, growing at **`19.28%` CAGR** to **`241.0B VND/yr` in `2029`**.

### 2.3 Serviceable Obtainable Market (`SOM`: PICK! HCMC Market Share)
- **2026 Current Baseline (`6 courts`)**: **`7.68B VND/yr`** (`7,680.00M VND/yr` = `640M VND/mo`, representing **`0.69%` of HCMC SAM** and **`5.41%` of Prime Catchment SAM**).
- **2029 Obtainable Multi-Venue Share (`20 courts` + community network)**: **`33.23B VND/yr`** (`33,231.02M VND/yr`, representing **`1.55%` of total HCMC SAM** and **`13.79%` of Prime Urban Catchment SAM**).
